## Character / object sheet (keep identical across all agents)
- **Sacabambaspis (recurring hero; draw it accurately)**: small fish, about 30 cm. A rounded, stiff **head shield** (about a third of the body length) of silvery grey-olive bone plates covered in tiny oak-leaf-shaped bumps; **two eyes at the very front of the head, close together, each in a small bony ring**; a **narrow slit mouth** at the front with a thin lower lip (rows of tiny scale-like plates); a few small gill openings along the side of the shield; rows of tiny pores on the shield; behind the shield a **flexible body covered in small diamond scales** that tapers to a **thin whip-like tail with a small tail fin at the tip**. **NO jaws, NO teeth, NO side/pectoral/pelvic fins, NO dorsal fin, NO scales on the shield, NO gills-flaps like a modern fish.** Colour: cool silver-grey armor `#9aa5a8`, olive-brown body `#7d7a5c`, warm yellow-ochre bumps. Mouth is shut or slightly open, never a toothy gape. It sits and swims low near the sea floor.
- **Helsinki museum model (for the meme units only)**: the same plan but cartoon-simple: smooth silvery body, big round googly doll eyes (white disc, black pupil) at the front, small dark triangular open mouth, tiny tail. Shown in a glass case with a plain label card (no text), or on a phone screen.
- **Haikouichthys / Myllokunmingia**: finger-sized, flattened, fish-like, soft, with a visible rod along the back, a pair of eyes, zigzag muscle blocks; pale pinkish-grey; NO armor.
- **Arandaspis**: like Sacabambaspis but with a long boat-shaped head shield, eyes at the front, no jaws, no side fins.
- **Astraspis / Eriptychius**: squat armored jawless fish, Astraspis shield covered in small star-shaped bumps.
- **Cameroceras-like nautiloid**: long straight cone shell with faint chamber lines, tentacles at the open end, cream with brown bands. **Trilobite**: oval three-lobed shell, grey-brown. **Early sea scorpion**: small segmented olive-brown body, paddle legs, no huge claws.
- **Dunkleosteus**: big armored head shield, bony blade jaws (no teeth), short stocky body, dark grey-blue, several meters long.
- **Lamprey**: eel-like, round sucker mouth ringed with small teeth. **Hagfish**: pink-grey, slimy, eel-like.
- **People**: only generic silhouettes (a sculptor at a workbench, museum visitors, children), cute proportions via `tools/body.py`, varied skin, dot eyes. **No portraits of real people** (Elga Mark-Kurik, the describers). The phone/laptop screens show shapes, never readable text.
- Sea floor is silty mud and sand; the water is warm and shallow; no plants taller than small algae mats; NO trees, NO insects, NO land animals in any Ordovician scene.
