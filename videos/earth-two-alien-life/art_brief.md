## Topic
The hunt for a second Earth: exoplanets, the habitable zone, how planets are found (wobble and transit), Kepler, Kepler-186f, Kepler-452b, Proxima Centauri b (flares, tidal locking, the twilight strip), TRAPPIST-1 (seven worlds), what makes Earth livable (magnetic field, plate tectonics), JWST and biosignatures (oxygen + methane), false alarms, the Fermi Paradox and the Great Filter, and the ending: Earth is precious, there is no planet B.
Modern anchors: a hand holding a warm coffee cup by a window at dawn (hook), a person looking up at a night sky from a balcony or a hill (silhouette from behind), a desk with star charts, a small observatory dome, a radio dish. NEVER a portrait of Fermi, Hanson or any real person; show silhouettes from behind, hands, desks, instruments.

## Character / object sheet (keep identical across all agents)
- **Modern person** (hook/ending): a calm adult in a dark-teal jacket and grey scarf, short dark hair, simple dot eyes; ids `mv-breathe-gazer`, `mv-nod-gazer`, `mv-arm-gazer`. Always from the side or behind.
- **Earth**: blue-green sphere with white cloud swirls and a thin bright rim; at night a faint web of golden city lights.
- **Red dwarf stars** (Kepler-186f, Proxima, TRAPPIST-1): deep orange-red glowing sphere, larger and warmer-looking in a planet's sky; **Sun-like G star** (Kepler-452b): gold-white. Glow = radial gradients (no ray fans, no filters).
- **Exoplanets**: Kepler-186f = rocky, slightly larger than Earth, amber-lit, ruddy-brown and grey-green; Kepler-452b = a bigger rocky super-Earth with blue-green oceans, pale clouds; Proxima b = rocky world with a day side lit orange, a dark night side and a thin glowing twilight ring (draw as a possibility, never a photo); TRAPPIST-1 = seven small rocky worlds in a tight line/arc around a small red star, each a slightly different colour (e is blue-green with a hint of ocean and cloud, as a possibility). Nothing is shown as a confirmed living world: no cities, no aliens, no creatures; hedged ideas (oceans, plants, life) appear only as soft suggestions (a shimmer on a water surface, a hint of green).
- **JWST**: golden hexagonal mirror segments, a diamond-shaped sunshield in layered silver-violet, drawn generically; **Kepler**: a cylindrical telescope with a large mirror and solar panels, generic.
- **Spectra**: a rainbow strip with a few dark gaps (no labels); **light curves**: a smooth line with a small dip (no numbers); **wobble**: a star and an unseen planet swinging around a shared centre of mass.
- No film references: nothing recognisable from any movie. No text, letters, numbers or equations inside the SVG (on-screen text comes from `labels`).
