## Topic
The history of how scientists and artists pictured extinct animals (paleoart), and how the pictures were corrected: the Crystal Palace dinosaur sculptures in a London park (bulky concrete Iguanodon with a nose horn, Megalosaurus); ancient people finding giant bones (myths, "dragon bones"); Mantell and the iguana-like teeth; the Bernissart coal mine skeletons and the thumb spike; the 1853 dinner inside the Iguanodon mould; the Bone Wars (two rival fossil hunters in the American West, quarries, wagons); Elasmosaurus with the head on the wrong end; Brontosaurus with the wrong boxy head; Stegosaurus with plates lying flat like roof tiles vs upright plates; the "second brain" myth; Charles R. Knight painting in a studio; sauropods wrongly standing in swamps; water pressure on a submerged chest; T. rex standing upright like a kangaroo with its tail on the ground vs horizontal pose; fossil trackways with no tail drags; Burgess Shale Cambrian animals (Anomalocaris pieces mistaken for a shrimp, a jellyfish and a sea cucumber, then reunited; Hallucigenia upside down then flipped, head at the other end); Deinonychus and the Dinosaur Renaissance; a 1990s film set feel (generic film camera, NO film logos, NO movie characters); Spinosaurus (sail, museum destroyed by bombing 1944 — show a damaged empty museum hall, no people hurt, no flags/symbols; old sail-backed biped version; new short-legged, paddle-tailed river version); shrink-wrapped skeleton look vs fleshy animals; a swan and a cat drawn shrink-wrapped (gentle, not gory); lips covering T. rex teeth; feathered Sinosauropteryx (ginger with banded tail, bandit mask), Velociraptor with feathered arms (turkey-sized), melanosomes under a microscope, Anchiornis (gray/black body, white wings with black tips, red crown), Microraptor (glossy iridescent black); scaly adult T. rex; sauropod hollow neck bones with air sacs; growth series (Pachycephalosaurus young to adult domes; Triceratops/Torosaurus frills); timeline of old vs new restorations; future scientists looking at today's pictures.
