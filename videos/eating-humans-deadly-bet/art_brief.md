Funeral custom = respectful abstraction: a mourning circle of silhouettes around a low fire at dusk, banana leaves, a wrapped bundle seen from far away, hands on shoulders, never the act itself. Patients = gentle, non-graphic: a figure leaning on a stick with small shake marks, a hand tremor, a seated figure with a laughing mouth and worried relatives. Brain = clean stylized cross-section / sponge-like holes, never realistic gore. Science scenes (microscope, flasks, mice-free) are clean and friendly. Prehistoric people = small cute silhouettes, no violence.
Continuity (same look every time): 
- **Fore highlands** = misty green mountain valleys, thatched round huts with smoke, tree-fern and banana leaves, warm dusk light. People = cute proportions (art_spec 6B), brown skin tones varied, simple bark-cloth/net-bag clothing, no body paint cliches, respectful.
- **Researchers** = 1950s: Zigas in khaki shirt and shorts with a notebook; Gajdusek in a white shirt with a microscope. Generic faces, no real likenesses. **Pathologists/scientists** = white coat, round glasses, friendly.
- **Brain** = lilac-pink rounded brain, sponge-hole pattern only in the damaged views, soft glow. **Prion** = a normal protein is a smooth teal helix/ribbon; a misfolded prion is the same ribbon bent and kinked, coral-red; a bent golden key is the visual metaphor in the "key" scenes. **Neurons** = branching cells with soft glow.
- **Cattle** = black-and-white cows in a green British field, feed sacks with a plain symbol (no text). **Chimpanzee** = friendly, in a clean cage-free lab room.
- **Mammoth** = brown woolly with curved tusks, **woolly rhino** = grey-brown with two horns, **red deer** = reddish with antlers (all at prehistoric steppe, cute and non-violent). Calorie scale = brass balance.
- Looks per unit: wide landscape, close-up of one object, cutaway/diagram, microscope view, split view; consecutive units change viewpoint and subject; at most 2 of 12 units share a backdrop kind; no concentric rings or radiating rays around a centre; no thick black outlines; bottom 200 px calm. `mv-` motion groups (art_spec 7) on creatures/parts; use at least 10 different centre-motion kinds across your batch and not more than 20% of one kind; do not give an object with moving parts a whole-object centre motion.
