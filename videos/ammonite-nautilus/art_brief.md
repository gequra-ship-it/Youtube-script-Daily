## Character / object sheet (keep identical across all agents)
- **Ammonite (recurring)**: flat coiled disc shell (planispiral, ~8 whorls visible), fine radial ribs, a keel along the outer edge, warm bronze `#c88a4a` base with pearly iridescent highlights (colour is an artistic choice; real colours are unknown, do not claim otherwise). The soft body is only partly known, so draw it simply: a short head with two large round eyes and a small cluster of ~8–10 short tentacles emerging from the shell opening; no suckers detail. Sutures drawn as wavy fractal lines only when the unit text is about sutures.
- **Nautilus (recurring hero)**: coiled shell, cream-white with warm reddish-brown tiger stripes on the outer side, pearly inside; a leathery hood on top of the head; a fringe of MANY thin tentacles (cirri) with NO suckers; one small round pinhole eye on each side (no lens, no pupil highlight, just a dark open pit); a funnel (a rolled tube) under the head. Hatchling: same shape, about 3 cm, a few chambers.
- **Coleoids (squid, cuttlefish, octopus)**: no external shell; draw the view that fits the unit; not fixed.
- **Dinosaurs** (S02 only): Tyrannosaurus and Triceratops as dignified silhouettes, no gore. Marine reptiles (plesiosaur) only as drifting skeletons in the marine-snow unit.
- **Asteroid**: dark rocky body, fiery glow, long trail.
- **Researcher (recurring, rare)**: adult, light-tan skin, dark ponytail, dot eyes, white lab coat with blue gloves; cute proportions via `tools/body.py` (kind='cute'). Desk scene (S50): same researcher, warm lamp, fossil and shell in hand.
- **Real people**: none; no portraits.
- Other animals/objects (plankton, fish, crustaceans, rock layers, ships) are NOT fixed: draw what fits THIS unit. Colours from `pal`; blues/teals dominate the sea; storm/space palettes for the impact.

## Care points
- No text or numbers anywhere in the SVG (sidecar `labels` only). Question marks, arrows and symbols without letters are fine.
- Death and decline (starving ammonites, dying plankton, the extinction itself): dignity, silhouette, mood only. No blood, no gore, no visible wounds.
- Depth/pressure and acid effects are hypotheses in the script: show them as clean diagrams, not as proven events.
- Do not draw the nautilus with suckers or with 8 arms; do not draw ammonites with the nautilus's tiger stripes.
- Ammonite hatchlings are about 1 mm: show them as tiny dots/specks next to a big object for scale; nautilus egg is about 3 cm, oval, leathery, attached to rock.
