Topic: "Neptune's Unsolved Mysteries". Neptune: most distant planet (30x farther from the Sun than Earth, the Sun a bright pinprick), Voyager 2 flew by once in 1989 (only visit); Uranus' odd orbit led two mathematicians to predict a hidden planet, found in 1846 within one degree of the predicted spot (first planet found with a pencil before the eye); deep blue colour only partly explained by methane (haze hypothesis); fastest winds in the solar system (near 2,000 km/h, westward equatorial jets) from almost no sunlight; Great Dark Spot (about as wide as Earth, seen 1989, gone by Hubble 1994); gives off 2.5x more heat than it receives vs. its twin Uranus (almost none); interior: hot slush mantle (water, ammonia, methane), possible diamond rain (hypothesis, lab-shock experiments); magnetic axis tilted 47 deg and off-centre (thin conductive shell hypothesis); Triton: retrograde orbit, captured Kuiper-belt dwarf planet, cold -235 C, cantaloupe-skin terrain, geysers of dark dust/nitrogen, will someday pass the Roche limit and break into a ring; Neptune's formation puzzle and the Nice model (Jupiter-Saturn 2:1 resonance kicked Neptune/Uranus outward through an icy belt). Hypothesis sentences ("may", "suspect", "model") are drawn ghosted/dreamlike/translucent, not as photos. NEVER draw real persons' faces, logos, flags, NASA insignia, text.
