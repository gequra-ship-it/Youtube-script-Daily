# art_brief — whale-fall (Miya's Note, ocean palette)
Read `tools/art_spec.md` fully (sections 1-9; vertical Shorts also 10; 12 = frog). Plan: `videos/<slug>/plan.json`; write `videos/<slug>/art3/<uid>.svg` + `.json` (only your own uids; skip `kind: chalk` units, they have no picture). Palette `ocean` (+ each unit's `pal`), almost all units are `night` (deep sea) and the first/last scene are `dusk` (sunlit surface). Own scratch folder for previews. No git. Do NOT draw the frog and do NOT write the `frog` sidecar key.
Topic: what happens when a whale dies and sinks (whale fall): the four stages (mobile scavengers, opportunists, sulfur lovers, reef), sharks, hagfish, giant isopods, bristle worms, zombie worm Osedax, chemosynthetic bacteria, mussels/clams, hydrothermal vents, whaling, carbon, the 1987 Alvin discovery. Numbers in the script are shown only through sidecar `labels` (no letters in the SVG). Hedged claims stay hedged (e.g. "may", "one hypothesis").
Continuity (same look every time it appears; copy shapes from the thumbnail drawing code `videos/whale-fall/thumb/make.py`, but in the thin-ink picture style of art_spec section 3, NOT the thick cartoon outline):
- **Whale** = large baleen/gray whale: slate blue-grey back, paler belly with throat grooves, small eye, long mouth line, broad tail flukes. Skeleton = pale bone-cream: long skull with separate lower jaw, row of vertebrae with spines, curved ribs shorter towards the tail, flipper with finger bones, tapering tail vertebrae. Carcass/blubber = pale grey-pink rounded mass, NO blood, NO gore, no open wounds; soften with silt and dark water.
- **Pacific sleeper shark** = slate-grey, thick rounded body, blunt round snout, small eyes, low small dorsal fins. **Hagfish** = pinkish-grey eel-like, no jaws, small eye, a pale slime strand. **Giant isopod** = pale lilac armoured oval, segmented back, two big dark eyes, many small legs. **Bristle worm** = small orange-pink segmented worm. **Osedax** = pale root stalk, bright red feathery plumes, roots growing into bone. **Bacterial mats** = thick pale white fluffy layers on bone. **Mussels/clams** = dark small shells in clusters. **Crabs**, small snails/limpets as needed.
- **Alvin-style submersible** = rounded white hull, small round viewport, bright lamp beams; no text/logo.
- Deep water: dark teal to navy gradient, floating marine snow (small pale dots), silt on the seabed; a lamp-lit pool of light is allowed. Surface scenes (dusk): warm sunset sky over calm sea, whale silhouette.
- Allowed looks per unit: wide seafloor, close-up of one animal, cut-away of bone with bacteria/worm roots, diagram-like cross-section, split view surface/seafloor. Never concentric rings or radiating rays around a centre object; no thick black outlines; bottom 200 px calm.
- Consecutive units change viewpoint and subject (wide -> close -> cut-away); at most 2 of 12 units share a backdrop kind; `mv-` motion groups (art_spec 7) on creatures: fins/tails `mv-chain`, plumes `mv-chain`/`mv-joint`, drifting snow `mv-cells`; use at least 10 different centre-motion kinds across your batch and not more than 20% of one kind; do not give an object with moving parts a whole-object centre motion.
