Topic: how scientists solved the end of the non-bird dinosaurs 66 million years ago. Giant reptiles ruled 160+ million years (longer than humans have existed); about three quarters of species vanished; a thin ribbon of clay (few cm) between rock layers: dinosaur fossils no longer appear above it; iridium (rare in Earth's crust, common in space rocks) found by Luis and Walter Alvarez 1980 (first measured at Gubbio, Italy; also Denmark, New Zealand); asteroid/comet idea; "where is the scar?"; erosion and continental drift hide craters; oil-company geologists saw a circular gravity/magnetic pattern under the Yucatan Peninsula and Gulf of Mexico -> Chicxulub crater, 150+ km wide, same age; 2016 drilling brought up shattered/melted rock; asteroid 10-15 km across at tens of thousands km/h hitting a shallow sea; energy like billions of atomic bombs (a comparison, not literal); cavity deeper than the crust is thick; earthquakes, tsunamis crossing the ocean; vaporized rock thrown above the atmosphere, falling back as glowing molten-glass beads (found in the layer) heating the sky like an oven; fires, soot in the clay; chain: minutes (blast, waves), hours (glass rain, heat), years (dark, cold); soot + sulfur haze, freezing twilight for years, photosynthesis fails, food chain collapses, giants (big plant eaters then hunters) starve first; Deccan Traps volcanoes in India (debate: how much they contributed; impact is agreed); survivors small: burrowing mammals, scavengers of dead plants/animals, small birds; birds are the living branch of feathered dinosaurs: a sparrow on a branch outside your window; mammals' descendants include us; a stray asteroid may be part of why we are here; resilience of life; a thin line of clay, a buried circle under the sea, a sparrow on a branch.
Visuals: quiet dawn landscape with sauropod and ceptoceratops herds (simple cute dinosaurs, round soft shapes), a cliff of rock layers with ONE thin glowing clay line (fossils only below), magnifier on the line, a periodic-table-style iridium tile, a rock from space, a globe with a dotted ring of sampling sites, a map outline of the Gulf of Mexico and Yucatan with a ring, gravity-map concentric colour rings, oil-rig drill rig, crater cross-section, drill core tubes, a huge rock hitting a shallow sea with a bright flash, shock rings, tsunami wave, falling glowing glass beads, a sky glowing like an oven, burning forest silhouettes, soot cloud, stacked chain cards (minutes/hours/years), grey twilight with dim sun, withered plants, empty food-chain pyramid, big dinosaur silhouette next to small mammal in burrow, volcano with lava rivers on a map of India, balance scale (impact vs volcanoes), a question mark over two clues, small mammals, a small bird, a sparrow on a branch with a ghostly T. rex shadow, a modern window with coffee mug and city sidewalk, sprouting green after ash.
