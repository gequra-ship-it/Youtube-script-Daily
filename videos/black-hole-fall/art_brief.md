the friend at a telescope on the mother ship watching you slow down "in thickening molasses"; light of your suit/beacon stretched: white -> dull orange -> deep crimson -> infrared/radio waves (draw wave stretching, colour shift, fading figure); you "freeze" at the threshold of the horizon (from the friend's view); your own wristwatch ticking normally, you floating weightless; the event horizon = a mathematical boundary (a thin glowing circle, NOT a solid wall; inside the horizon: space and time swap roles (a one-way arrow/road to the centre), the outside universe squeezed into a bright circular window behind you while darkness opens ahead, blueshift; the singularity as a moment in time on a calendar/timeline (not a place); spaghettification (your body as a stretching thread/ribbon — cartoon, NO gore, simple dignified silhouette, calm), shredded suit shown only as drifting fabric strips; the information paradox: your atoms/DNA/memories as icons (DNA helix, atom, photo/brain-glow) heading in; information possibly imprinted in the radiation (scrambled pixels/ripples); the unknown beyond the horizon (a closed vault, a locked door in the dark; honest "we cannot verify"); final: a person looking up at a starry night sky, black patches between stars.
