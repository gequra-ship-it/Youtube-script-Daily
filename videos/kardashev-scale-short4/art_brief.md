Topic: "How Powerful Is Humanity? The Kardashev Scale": civilizations ranked by energy use (Soviet astronomer Nikolai Kardashev, 1960s): Type One = all energy of its home planet, Type Two = all power of its star, Type Three = a whole galaxy. Humanity ~Type 0.7 (Carl Sagan's estimate). Freeman Dyson's swarm of solar collectors around a star ("Dyson sphere", only an idea, never found). Type Three may tap rotating supermassive black holes (theory). The silent sky (hypothesis: a dangerous step between Type 0 and 1). Everything beyond humanity is imagination: draw it as a clearly imagined/speculative picture, never as a photo of something real.
Continuity (same look every time): **Earth** = blue-teal globe with soft green continents and white swirls, thin atmosphere rim; **night-side Earth** = dark globe with warm golden city-light clusters; **star/Sun** = glowing warm-white to orange disc with soft corona, no concentric rings; **Dyson swarm** = a star wrapped by a loose cloud of many small dark-blue solar panels with gold edges catching the light, light streaming through gaps (never a solid shell, never concentric rings); **ladder / three rungs** = a tall glowing gold ladder with planet, star, galaxy on rungs when used; **galaxy** = tilted spiral with soft gold core and teal arms; **black hole** = black disc, thin gold rim, tilted warm accretion ring; **power grid** = thin glowing gold lines linking cities and plants across a dark continent; **wind turbines / solar panels / hydro dam / geothermal vent** = simple cute shapes; **people** = cute 5.5-head figures, no face detail (body.py rules); **Kardashev** = no face: a small desk silhouette with a radio dish, nothing else; **Sagan** = no face, a silhouette at a telescope; **alien civilization** = tiny glowing city lights on a far planet, never creatures with faces. Large readable shapes, depth (front/middle/back), one clear focal object, soft glow.
No letters or numbers inside SVG (use sidecar `labels`). Do not draw the frog (builder adds it where `frog_hint` is true; do NOT write a `frog` key, art_spec 12 wins).
