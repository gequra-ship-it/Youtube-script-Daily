Topic: 1995 Minnesota students net frogs with extra legs; pollution suspects fail in labs; young researcher Pieter Johnson surveys ponds in Santa Clara County, California and finds cysts of the flatworm Ribeiroia ondatrae; three hosts: ramshorn snail (parasite sterilizes it, makes clones) -> free-swimming fork-tailed cercariae -> tadpole limb buds (encysts, scrambles limb building; extra, missing, fused legs) -> heron/egret/duck (crippled frogs are easy prey; adult worm in bird gut; eggs in droppings -> new pond); museum specimens back to 1946; fertilizer runoff -> algae bloom -> more snails -> more parasites; honest limits (not the only threat); web of life lesson.
**SENSITIVE-ISH, rules**: cartoon deformed frogs are gentle and cute-creepy, never gore: NO blood, wounds, open bodies, dissection close-ups, guts; the heron catching a frog is a beak-and-splash silhouette, no bite details; cysts = clean amber beads; the bird gut = a soft cutaway diagram.
Continuity (same look every time):
- **Frog** = northern leopard frog: olive-green with darker round spots, cream belly, big round eyes, cute proportions (not the mascot: no notebook, no mascot look). **Deformed frog** = same frog with 2-4 extra thin hind legs from the hip (varied), sad worried eyes; or missing hind legs (crawling on front arms), fused legs; shown gently. **Tadpole** = brown-green oval body, flat tail, tiny hind-limb buds as small bumps near the tail base (glow softly when the story is about them).
- **Ramshorn snail** = flat coiled spiral shell, reddish-brown, small tentacles. **Ribeiroia** = tiny pale pink-beige flatworm (leaf-shaped, goggle-free, no face needed); **cercaria** = a tiny bead body with a long forked tail, glassy white-blue, shown in swarms; **miracidium** = tiny fuzzy oval swimmer; **cyst/metacercaria** = amber round bead; **eggs** = tiny pale dots.
- **Heron** = blue-grey heron with long neck and dagger beak (also egret = white, duck = brown); reed bed at dusk.
- **Students** = small cute kids with butterfly nets and a teacher on a green marsh (1995, daytime, summer). **Young researcher** = cute proportions, waders, cap, notebook, clipboard; generic face, no real likeness. Scientists = white coat, round glasses.
- **Pond** = still green water, reeds, dragonflies, lily pads; underwater views with soft light shafts. **Pollution suspects** = icons as objects (pesticide jug without text, barrel with a plain hazard symbol, sun with UV rays, cloud/ozone hole, metal ingot), lab = flasks, tadpole tanks.
- **Farm** = crop field, tractor, fertilizer sacks (plain symbol, no text), rain clouds, runoff stream into a pond with green algae blanket. **Museum** = shelves of jars with small frog silhouettes, labels without text.
- Looks per unit: wide landscape, close-up of one creature, underwater, cutaway/diagram, microscope view, split view; consecutive units change viewpoint and subject; at most 2 of 12 units share a backdrop kind; no concentric rings or radiating rays around a centre; no thick black outlines; bottom 200 px calm. `mv-` motion groups on creatures/parts; use at least 10 different centre-motion kinds across your batch and not more than 20% of one kind; do not give an object with moving parts a whole-object centre motion.
