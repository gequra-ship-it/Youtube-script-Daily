Topic: "The Beetle That Hunts Frogs Alive": larva of the ground beetle Epomis (Israel, Wizen & Gasith 2011 lab study) lures a young frog/toad by waving antennae and jaws like a worm, dodges the tongue, clamps its hooked jaws on the amphibian, feeds (sucks body fluids, later chews, only bones remain), moults through three stages, pupates in soil, becomes an adult beetle that jumps on an amphibian's back and bites; science side notes: frog vision reacts to tiny moving dots, young amphibians 2-3 weeks after metamorphosis, a toad swallowed a larva for ~2 hours then spat it out, Red Queen idea (two runners on a treadmill), where the beetles live (map-like stylised world, no letters), nights at damp shelters under stones and old wood.
Visuals: damp pond bank at night with moon, reeds, stones, wood debris, mud; a calm frog/toad sitting side-on watching; close-ups of the larva (pale segmented body, big curved hooked jaws, two waving antennae, six legs); frog eye with reflex dot; tongue flick (cartoon, pink, no gore); lab-ish scenes (Petri-like arena, notebook, stopwatch, researcher as simple dignified silhouette); bones pile as a calm symbol (stylised small skeleton, no flesh, no blood); soil chamber with a pupa; adult Epomis beetle (shiny metallic dark green/blue with pale rim, long legs); wild night scene with a flashlight beam; treadmill/runners metaphor; stylised map; sunrise/pond ending.
Continuity (same look every time): **Larva** = pale cream-tan segmented caterpillar-like body, brownish head with two long curved hooked jaws and two thin antennae (waving), six small legs. **Adult beetle** = glossy dark teal/black shell with a thin pale-yellow rim, long legs, jaws forward. **Frog/toad (victim)** = smooth cute-cartoon jade-green frog, side profile, big glossy eye, closed wavy mouth (flustered face in trouble scenes), NOT warts, NOT gross; toad variant = rounder, tan-brown, same cute style. The channel mascot (note-frog) is a different character: the builder adds it where `frog_hint` is true; do NOT draw it and do NOT write a `frog` key (art_spec 12 wins). **People** = simple dignified silhouettes (cute proportions), dot eyes, varied skin.
Never: blood, torn flesh, open wounds, visible swallowing of a living victim; show the feeding only through symbols (larva clinging at the frog's lip/side, a worried frog, then a small clean skeleton). Death only via symbols. The tongue is a simple pink ribbon.
