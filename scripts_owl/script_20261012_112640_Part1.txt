- 영어 자막: The Thunder God’s Hidden Flaw
- 영어 TTS: When ancient Norsemen looked up at a raging storm, they did not see a benevolent protector or a philosophical sage. They saw the violent fury of Thor, the red-bearded god whose bronze chariot shook the very foundations of the cosmos. Yet, buried deep within the surviving medieval manuscripts lies a bizarre paradox that modern culture completely overlooks. The most terrifying warrior of the northern pantheon was also an astonishingly gullible figure, repeatedly deceived, mocked, and outsmarted by his bitterest enemies.

- 영어 TTS: To understand how the master of thunder became the eternal victim of ancient deceptions, we must abandon modern adaptations and turn directly to the primary sources. The Poetic Edda and the Prose Edda, compiled centuries ago in medieval Iceland, contain the true oral memories of the Viking age. These verses describe a deity whose physical might was virtually infinite, but whose mental faculty was astonishingly blunt. Whenever a situation required subtle trickery rather than bone-crushing force, Thor stumbled into traps that even a mortal child might have avoided.

- 영어 TTS: The pantheon of Asgard was fundamentally divided between two radically opposed principles of power. On one side stood Odin the Allfather, a terrifying sorcerer who sacrificed his own eye for forbidden occult wisdom, a master of psychological manipulation who never fought fairly. On the other side stood his son Thor, who disdained all forms of magical deceit and measured every challenge by the weight of iron. Odin operated through whispers, betrayal, and hidden oaths, while Thor knew only a direct line connecting his hammer to an opponent's skull.

Because Thor possessed the ultimate physical solution to every conflict, he never developed the intellectual patience required to identify a cunning lie before it struck him.
