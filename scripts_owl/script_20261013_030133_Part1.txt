When physicists fired solid particles of matter—electrons—through two narrow barriers, they expected two neat lines to appear on the detector screen. That is precisely what classical mechanics and common sense demanded from solid physical objects. Instead, what materialized was a stunning interference pattern of alternating light and dark bands, the unmistakable signature of a fluid wave. How could solid, discrete units of matter behave like rippling waves in an ocean? The electrons were not passing through one slit or the other; remarkably, a single electron was somehow passing through both slits simultaneously, interfering with its own ghost.

Reality refused to reveal its hidden path; the mere act of measurement fundamentally transformed the physical state of the universe. This phenomenon, known as the collapse of the wave function, proved that subatomic reality does not exist in a definite state until it is forced to do so.

- 영어 자막: Heisenberg's Shattering Principle
- 영어 TTS: In nineteen twenty-seven, German physicist Werner Heisenberg formulated the mathematical death blow to absolute divine knowledge: the Uncertainty Principle. Heisenberg proved that it is physically impossible to simultaneously measure the exact position and the exact momentum of a subatomic particle. The more precisely you pin down where an electron is, the more furiously and unpredictably its velocity fluctuates into total obscurity. This is not a limitation of human technology, nor is it a temporary flaw in our laboratory equipment. It is an intrinsic, unbreakable constitutional law woven into the fabric of the cosmos itself. The universe possesses an absolute barrier beyond which information simply does not exist.
- 한글 번역: 1927년, 독일의 물리학자 베르너 하이젠베르크는 절대적인 신적 지식을 향한 수학적 치명타를 공식화했습니다. 바로 불확정성 원리입니다. 하이젠베르크는 아원자 입자의 정확한 위치와 정확한 운동량을 동시에 측정하는 것이 물리적으로 불가능하다는 것을 증명했습니다. 전자의 위치를 정밀하게 파악하면 할수록, 그 속도는 완전한 불명확성 속에서 더욱 격렬하고 예측할 수 없게 요동칩니다. 이것은 인류 기술의 한계도, 실험실 장비의 일시적인 결함도 아닙니다. 그것은 우주 자체의 구조에 짜인 본질적이고 깨뜨릴 수 없는 헌법적 법칙입니다. 우주는 정보 자체가 단순히 존재하지 않는 절대적인 장벽을 지니고 있습니다.

But quantum mechanics dictates that an electron does not have a precise position and momentum at the same time. The information does not hide in a secret pocket; it literally does not exist in nature. Therefore, if the information does not exist, even an infinite, omnipotent God cannot know it without violating logic itself. The subatomic realm is inherently blind to absolute foreknowledge.

- 영어 TTS: For millennia, theologians argued from the Cosmological Argument: Everything that begins to exist must have a cause, and God is the ultimate cause. Every domino in the cosmic sequence falls because an earlier domino struck it with mechanical precision. But at the quantum scale, this revered law of cause and effect simply evaporates into thin air. Particles decay, transition, and materialize without any preceding trigger or hidden mechanism, striking a devastating blow to the concept of a divine prime mover.
- 한글 번역: 수천 년 동안 신학자들은 우주론적 논증을 통해 주장해 왔습니다. - 영어 자막: The Spontaneous Decay
- 영어 TTS: Consider the radioactive decay of an unstable atom, such as carbon-fourteen or uranium-two-thirty-eight. If you place a solitary unstable nucleus in a sealed, isolated chamber, you can calculate its half-life with extraordinary statistical accuracy. You know that within a specific timeframe, half of a vast collection of such atoms will decay. But if you ask: Why did this specific atom decay at this exact microsecond while its neighbor remained completely stable? Quantum mechanics provides a shocking, chilling answer: There is no reason. There is no internal clock ticking down, no external pressure applied, and no hidden divine finger pulling a cosmic trigger. It happens out of pure, uncaused, irreducible spontaneity.
- 한글 번역: 탄소-14나 우라늄-238과 같은 불안정한 원자의 방사성 붕괴를 생각해 보십시오. 밀폐되고 고립된 방에 하나의 불안정한 원자핵을 놓아둔다면, 여러분은 엄청난 통계적 정확도로 그 반감기를 계산할 수 있습니다. 특정한 시간 프레임 안에서 수많은 원자 중 절반이 붕괴할 것이라는 사실을 압니다. 하지만 "왜 바로 이 특정 원자가 이웃한 원자는 완벽히 안정된 상태로 남아있는 동안 바로 이 마이크로초에 붕괴했는가?"라고 묻는다면, 양자역학은 충격적이고 섬뜩한 답을 내놓습니다. "아무런 이유도 없다." 내부에서 째깍거리는 시계도, 외부에서 가해진 압력도, 우주적 방아쇠를 당기는 신의 숨겨진 손가락도 없습니다. 그것은 순수하고, 원인 없는, 더 이상 환원할 수 없는 자발성에서 일어납니다.

[장면 10]
- 시각 자료: 텅 빈 절대 암흑의 진공 공간에서 푸른빛과 붉은빛의 가상 입자 쌍이 폭발하듯 솟아올랐다 충돌해 사라지는 애니메이션
- 영어 자막: Quantum Fluctuations in the Void
- 영어 TTS: Perhaps the most staggering discovery of modern quantum field theory is that the vacuum of empty space is not actually empty. If you strip away every star, every atom, and every photon of radiation, the remaining void seethes with violent activity. Due to the uncertainty relation between energy and time, subatomic particles can literally borrow immense energy from nothingness, pop into physical reality for a trillionth of a second, and violently annihilate each other back into nothingness.
