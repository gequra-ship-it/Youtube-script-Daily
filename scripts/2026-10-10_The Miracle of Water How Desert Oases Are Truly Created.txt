- 영어 TTS: To understand an oasis, we must first examine why deserts are so dry in the first place. In these zones, dry air sinks from the upper atmosphere, preventing clouds and moisture from condensing. Rain can be absent for years, and surface temperatures can soar beyond fifty degrees Celsius. [장면 8]

- 영어 TTS: As this subterranean water flows beneath the desert, it encounters different types of geological strata. - 영어 자막: The Artesian Phenomenon
- 영어 TTS: Natural pressure inside an artesian aquifer acts like an overinflated water balloon. The weight of the water upstream in the mountains creates continuous hydrostatic force pressing down on the system. If a natural fracture, earthquake fault, or geological fissure cracks open the overlying rock, magic happens. The pressurized water explodes upward toward the surface without requiring any mechanical pump. This natural upward fountain is how many legendary oases are born.

- 시각 자료: 탄소 연대 측정을 표현하는 실험실 그래픽과 수포 분자 구조

- 영어 TTS: Isotopic analysis allows scientists to accurately determine the exact age of this fossil water.
