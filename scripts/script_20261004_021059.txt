- 영어 자막: Where modern physics completely breaks down.

- 영어 자막: The battle over lost cosmic information.
- 영어 TTS: This clash sparks the famous Information Paradox. Does the information of everything that fell inside disappear forever? Stephen Hawking suggested black holes slowly evaporate, leaking radiation and confusing physicists for decades.
