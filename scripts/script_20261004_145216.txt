- 영어 자막: Phase One: The Scavengers
- 영어 TTS: Within mere hours, chemical cues from the carcass drift for miles. This initiates Phase One: The Mobile Scavenger Phase. Giant sleeper sharks, primitive hagfish, and deep-sea crabs converge from the darkness for an unprecedented feast.

[슬라이드 6]

- 영어 자막: Tearing Through the Flesh
- 영어 TTS: Hagfish burrow directly into the blubber, consuming the body from the inside out, while sharks rip away massive chunks of meat. Working together, these ravenous creatures can devour up to sixty kilograms of tissue every single day.
- 한글 번역: 먹장어는 두꺼운 지방층을 직접 파고들어 몸속부터 먹어 치우고, 상어들은 거대한 살점을 뜯어냅니다. 이 굶주린 생물들은 협력하듯 매일 최대 60킬로그램의 조직을 먹어 치웁니다.

- 영어 자막: Stripped Bare in Months
- 영어 TTS: You might expect a giant to take decades to decompose, but this brutal frenzy strips away nearly ninety percent of the soft tissue in just a few months to two years, leaving behind a magnificent skeleton.

[슬라이드 8]

- 영어 자막: Phase Two: The Opportunists
- 영어 TTS: With the heavy meat gone, Phase Two begins: The Opportunist Phase. Now, thousands of smaller creatures, like polychaete worms, brittle stars, and tiny snails, colonize the exposed bones and the rich organic sediments left behind.

- 영어 자막: An Oasis of Biodiversity
- 영어 TTS: In this stage, the sea floor around the whale becomes one of the densest biological hotspots on Earth. For up to four years, these tiny scavengers glean every microscopic morsel, preparing the stage for the strangest transformation yet.

[슬라이드 10]

- 영어 자막: Phase Three: Powered by Chemicals
- 영어 TTS: Welcome to Phase Three: The Sulfophilic, or sulfur-loving, phase. Whales have bones filled with rich lipids, accounting for up to sixty percent of their dry weight. As bacteria break down these fats, they release toxic hydrogen sulfide.
- 한글 번역: 3단계인 '황-친화성(Sulfophilic) 단계'에 오신 것을 환영합니다. 고래 뼈는 건조 중량의 최대 60%에 달하는 풍부한 지질로 가득 차 있습니다. 박테리아가 이 지방을 분해하면서 유독한 황화수소를 방출하기 시작합니다.

[슬라이드 11]

- 영어 자막: Enter the "Zombie Worms"
- 영어 TTS: This toxic environment gives rise to one of nature's weirdest organisms: Osedax, famously known as the Zombie Worm. Lacking a mouth, gut, or eyes, these bizarre creatures look like blooming red flowers growing straight out of the bone.

- 영어 자막: Guardians of the Climate
