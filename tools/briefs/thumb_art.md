The owner's four favourites, and WHY they work:
1. `AN INVISIBLE WALL`: film homage to **The Truman Show** (boat reaches the sky wall, stairs up to a glowing door). You recognise the film, then wonder why it is here.
2. `3 HOURS = 23 YEARS`: film homage to **Interstellar** (huge wave, small astronaut, ringed black hole).

Common: big stacked text top-left (2-4 words, 2-3 lines, Fredoka Bold, white with thick dark outline, ONE key word/number in yellow #F2D27A), ONE big clear metaphor picture on the right/bottom (about half the frame, 3-4 elements, soft glow on the focal object, small sparkles, a small figure for scale if it fits), bottom-right corner empty. Spend your effort on the picture; take as many drawing passes as needed.
