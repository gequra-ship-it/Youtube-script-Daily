# Brief for the Opus thumbnail agent (guideline 16-6)
You make THREE YouTube thumbnails (A, B, C; 1920x1080 PNG) for ONE video of the channel Miya's Note. Read first: `docs/guideline/16-thumbnail.md` (esp. 16-2, 16-6), `tools/thumb_lib.py` (page/text helpers), `tools/figlib.py` (every visible hand with fingers = `hand(...)`, 12 poses; `tools/hands.py` only when matching an old thumbnail), `tools/art_spec.md` section 6A/6B if you draw people (cute proportions, body.arm).

## What a good thumbnail is here (the owner's words: "it must be fun and eye-catching")
The owner's four favourites, and WHY they work:
1. `AN INVISIBLE WALL`: film homage to **The Truman Show** (boat reaches the sky wall, stairs up to a glowing door). You recognise the film, then wonder why it is here.
2. `3 HOURS = 23 YEARS`: film homage to **Interstellar** (huge wave, small astronaut, ringed black hole).
3. `IT FEEDS THE DEEP`: **intuitive**: a whale skeleton on the sea floor with crabs and worms around it; you understand it without reading.
4. `A PROTEIN THAT KILLS`: **intuitive**: a purple brain full of holes, a neon helix, a red bent protein.
Common: big stacked text top-left (2-4 words, 2-3 lines, Fredoka Bold, white with thick dark outline, ONE key word/number in yellow #F2D27A), ONE big clear metaphor picture on the right/bottom (about half the frame, 3-4 elements, soft glow on the focal object, small sparkles, a small figure for scale if it fits), bottom-right corner empty. Colours come from the script's subject (not always night blue); the topic palette (tools/palettes.py) is only a skeleton: change or add colours as suits the picture (guideline 3-5).
Make the three DIFFERENT: at least one **film homage** (pick a famous film scene whose composition really matches this video's topic; borrow composition/idea only, redraw it yourself, no actor faces, characters, logos or film-title lettering) and at least one **intuitive** picture. Text is English only, 2-5 words, facts that appear in the script (read `videos/<slug>/script.py`; numbers only if the script has them; hedged claims stay hedged).

## Quality bar (the previous versions were rejected: "boring, not eye-catching")
Previous attempts were quick flat shapes: tiny subject, no light, no detail. Do better: the subject is LARGE and detailed (shading, rim light, texture, small secondary details like crabs around the whale bones), the lighting has a clear light source and glow, the composition has depth (foreground / middle / background). Spend your effort on the picture; take as many drawing passes as needed. Draw as SVG inside `videos/<slug>/thumb/{A,B,C}.html` (generated by a `make.py` you keep in that folder using `tools/thumb_lib.py`; no external CDN or images; fonts: `common.css` + `Fredoka-700.woff2` already there).
