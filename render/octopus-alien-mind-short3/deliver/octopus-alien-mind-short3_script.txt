===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Aquarium staff know the octopus as an escape artist. There are many accounts of octopuses leaving their tanks at night, crossing wet floors to reach other tanks, and returning home before morning. The most famous case is Inky.

Inky lived at the National Aquarium of New Zealand in 2016. Staff believe Inky slipped through a small gap at the top of his tank, crossed about eight feet of floor, and squeezed into a drainpipe roughly fifty meters long that led to the sea.

Octopuses also seem to learn from cause and effect. At the Sea Star Aquarium in Coburg, Germany, an octopus named Otto reportedly squirted a jet of water at a bright lamp above his tank, apparently to switch it off. The short circuit cut the power to the whole aquarium, and the lamps were later hung higher.

In laboratory studies, octopuses have solved a number of puzzles. An octopus given a crab inside a clear jar with a screw lid has been reported to learn how to unscrew the lid. These stories point to problem solving, although many are individual observations rather than large controlled studies.

Octopuses can even tell individual humans apart. At the Seattle Aquarium, researchers worked with eight giant Pacific octopuses and two people in the same T-shirt. One person always fed them, and the other touched them with a bristly stick. After two weeks, the octopuses behaved differently toward the two. The study was small, and it is not clear which cues they used.

In a study published in 2009, researchers in Indonesia described veined octopuses carrying coconut shell halves across the sea floor in an awkward, stilt-walking way. When a threat appeared, they assembled the two halves into a shelter. The researchers considered this the first documented case of tool use in an invertebrate.

Another sign of a curious mind may be play. Researchers reported that captive octopuses blew floating plastic pill bottles toward a stream of water, let them drift back, and blew them out again, over and over. Scientists described this as play-like behavior. Proving play in animals is difficult.
