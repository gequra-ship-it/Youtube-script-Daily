=== TITLE ===
How a Tiny Beetle Larva Turns Frogs Into Its Meal

=== DESCRIPTION ===
What happens when an insect refuses to be a frog's dinner? The larva of the Epomis ground beetle lures frogs and toads, dodges the tongue in almost every encounter, and feeds on the animal that came to eat it.

In this video, we look at how frogs see their prey, the lure and the double-hooked jaws, the 382 lab trials from Israel, the adult beetle's night ambush, and the questions scientists still cannot answer. We also meet other small hunters that break the size rules.
