=== TITLE ===
What Happens if You Detonate a Nuke in Space?

=== DESCRIPTION ===
No sound, no shockwave, no mushroom cloud: what a nuclear blast does in the vacuum of space, and what the 1962 Starfish Prime test did to Earth and its satellites.
