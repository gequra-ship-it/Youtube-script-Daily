=== TITLE ===
Falling from Ten Kilometers: The Extreme Science of Survival

=== DESCRIPTION ===
What would happen if you fell from ten kilometers up with no parachute? Thin air, freezing cold, and a fall of roughly two hundred kilometers per hour would all be against you, yet a handful of people have survived falls from airliner heights.

In this video, we follow the fall second by second: the time of useful consciousness, drag and terminal velocity, the spread-eagle position, why stopping distance decides everything, and what the cases of Vesna Vulovic and Juliane Koepcke suggest about trees, snow, and wreckage.
