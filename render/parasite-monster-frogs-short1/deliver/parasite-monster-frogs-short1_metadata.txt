=== TITLE ===
The Frogs With Too Many Legs

=== DESCRIPTION ===
In 1995, students in Minnesota netted frogs with extra legs. Why? The first suspects were pollutants, and they all failed.

SOURCES
- Johnson et al. (2003), Limb deformities as an emerging parasitic disease in amphibians: evidence from museum specimens and resurvey data, Conservation Biology
- Johnson & Chase (2004), Parasites in the food web: linking amphibian malformations and aquatic eutrophication, Ecology Letters
- Johnson et al. (2007), Aquatic eutrophication promotes pathogenic infection in amphibians, PNAS
