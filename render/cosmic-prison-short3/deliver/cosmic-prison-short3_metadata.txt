=== TITLE ===
The Most Humbling Photo Ever Taken: The Pale Blue Dot

=== DESCRIPTION ===
On February 14, 1990, Voyager 1 turned its camera back toward home from 3.7 billion miles away. Earth appeared as a pale blue dot just 0.12 of a pixel across, caught in a ray of scattered sunlight. Carl Sagan, who pushed for the image, later named his 1994 book after it.
