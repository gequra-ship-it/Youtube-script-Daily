Hello out there?
Look down at your own hand.
Did you know that
the simple fact you
exist right now is
a powerful clue that
extraterrestrial life is real?
Most people spend their
lives searching the sky
for flying saucers or
listening for mysterious
radio beeps.
But they are missing
one of the most
basic ideas in probability.
In science, there is
a huge gap between
zero and one.
Zero means an event
is impossible under the
laws of nature.
One means it is possible.
Before life began on
Earth, we had no
proof that dead chemical
matter could ever wake
up and become a
living organism.
But the moment biology
ignited on our planet,
that zero permanently turned
into a one.
Our home galaxy alone
contains up to four
hundred billion stars,
and the observable universe
holds somewhere between
two hundred billion and
two trillion galaxies.
Surveys suggest that,
on average, every star
hosts at least one planet.
NASA's Kepler space
telescope alone found
thousands of planets,
and some are roughly
Earth-sized and orbit in
their star's habitable
zone, where liquid water
could exist.
There is also a
guiding rule in science
called the Copernican
Principle.
It says that Earth
is not in a
special, privileged place
in the universe,
and that the same
laws of physics apply
everywhere.
Now imagine walking along
thousands of miles of
sandy coastline.
You pick up a
single scoop of wet
sand, and inside it
is a living crab.
Would you ever believe
it is the only
living creature on the
entire planet?
Earth is that single
scoop of sand.
The cosmic kitchen is
stocked with the same
carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen
everywhere we look.
If the laws of
physics are the same
across every galaxy,
then a natural chemical
process that worked once
is very likely to
work again on countless
other worlds.
That is the heart
of the argument.
One example cannot tell
us exactly how common
life is, and we
have not found a
second one yet.
But you are not
a cosmic exception.
You may be the
working blueprint.

may be the strongest
clue we have that
we are not alone.
