=== TITLE ===
Why Deep Sea Creatures Grow Into Giants

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Why do animals in the cold, dark, food-poor deep sea grow into giants? Cold, metabolism, oxygen and pressure are the leading ideas, and we weigh each as a hypothesis.
