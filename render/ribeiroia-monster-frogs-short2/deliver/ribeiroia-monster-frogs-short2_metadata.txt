=== TITLE ===
Why a Parasite Wants Frogs to Be Eaten

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ribeiroia ondatrae travels from snails to tadpoles to herons. Malformed frogs jump shorter and swim slower, and scientists have proposed that this helps the parasite reach the birds it needs.
