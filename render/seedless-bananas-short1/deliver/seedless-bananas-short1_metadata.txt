=== TITLE ===
Why Bananas Have No Seeds

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Cut open a banana and you will not find seeds that can grow. This Short follows the rare chromosome accident, the soft seedless fruit, and the suckers and tissue culture that let people grow bananas without seeds.
