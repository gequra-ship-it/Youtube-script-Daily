===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

What if it rained, on and off, for a million years or more? About 234 million years ago, something like that really happened to our planet.

Huge volcanic eruptions, in rocks that are now part of western Canada, released carbon dioxide. The planet warmed by at least 3 to 4 degrees Celsius.

A warmer ocean sent more water into the sky. Across the supercontinent Pangaea, dry lands gained rivers, lakes, and wetlands.

The proof is in the rocks: river sands in Germany, wet clays in England, and amber in Italy holding the oldest known arthropods trapped in resin.

In the oceans, about one third of species were lost. But modern-style coral reefs began, and conifer forests spread.

And on land, a group of small, rare animals got their big chance: the dinosaurs. The full story is on Miya's Lab.
