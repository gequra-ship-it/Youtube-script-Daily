=== TITLE ===
Why Left-Handers Survive: The Fighting Hypothesis

=== DESCRIPTION ===
About one person in ten is left-handed, and the ratio seems older than our own species. If sharing the same hand helps people cooperate, why didn't left-handers disappear? One idea, the fighting hypothesis, says a rare left hand gives the surprise advantage in close combat, and that cooperation and competition may balance at roughly ten percent.

Based on the Abrams and Panaggio model (2012) and the Faurie and Raymond study of traditional societies (2005). The full video on Miya's Lab covers the whole story.
