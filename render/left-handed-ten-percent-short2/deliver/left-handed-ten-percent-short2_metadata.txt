=== TITLE ===
Handedness Starts Before Birth: Ultrasounds and Spinal Cords

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Handedness shows up surprisingly early: in Belfast ultrasound studies, about 85 percent of fetuses at ten weeks moved their right arm more, and the thumb a fetus sucked often matched the hand the child later preferred. A 2017 study in Germany found genes more active on one side of the fetal spinal cord from the eighth week.

No single left-handed gene exists: two left-handed parents have a left-handed child about 26 percent of the time, and identical twins disagree in roughly one pair in five. The full video on Miya's Lab follows the whole story.
