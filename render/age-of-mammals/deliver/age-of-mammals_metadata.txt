=== TITLE ===
The Age of Mammals: From the Fall of Dinosaurs to Modern Humans

=== DESCRIPTION ===
After an asteroid ended the age of dinosaurs about 66 million years ago, small, furry survivors inherited the Earth. Follow the mammals through the whole Cenozoic era, from the first shrew-like survivors to the people reading this now.

In this video, we watch an asteroid change the world, see mammals diversify into whales, bats and primates, follow cooling climates and spreading grasslands that reshaped teeth, hooves and hunters, and trace how walking upright, stone tools, a hungry brain, fire, farming and writing led to us.
