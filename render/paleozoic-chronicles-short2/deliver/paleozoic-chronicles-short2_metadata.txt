=== TITLE ===
Your Jaw, Arm and Backbone Came From Ancient Fish

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Your backbone, your jaw and the bones of your arm were first tried out by animals that lived hundreds of millions of years ago. This Short follows them from Pikaia and the first jaws to Tiktaalik, early four-limbed animals and the amniotic egg.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, a journey through the Paleozoic Era.
