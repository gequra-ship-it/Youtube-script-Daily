=== TITLE ===
The Pig-Sized Survivor of the Great Dying

=== DESCRIPTION ===
About 252 million years ago, volcanic eruptions in Siberia set off the Great Dying, the worst mass extinction in the history of animal life. A tusked plant-eater called Lystrosaurus came out on top.
