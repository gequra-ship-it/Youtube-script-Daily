=== TITLE ===
The Great Dying: Earth's Worst Mass Extinction

=== DESCRIPTION ===
About 252 million years ago, volcanic eruptions in Siberia set off the worst crisis in the history of animal life. Roughly 80 percent or more of marine species disappeared, along with about 70 percent of land vertebrate species. This Short follows the Great Dying and the survivor Lystrosaurus.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, a journey through the Paleozoic Era.
