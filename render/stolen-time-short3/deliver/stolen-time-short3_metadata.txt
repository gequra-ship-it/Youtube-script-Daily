The Spinning Black Hole a Planet Could Survive

=== DESCRIPTION ===
A planet cannot survive close to an ordinary, non-rotating black hole. This Short explains why a stable ocean world would need a huge, rapidly spinning Kerr black hole, and what Kip Thorne calculated about the spin.
