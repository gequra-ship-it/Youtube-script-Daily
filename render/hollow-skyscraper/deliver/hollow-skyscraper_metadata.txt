=== TITLE ===
Why the Hollow Burj Khalifa Never Collapses

=== DESCRIPTION ===
How can a tower that is mostly air stand 828 meters tall? Burj Khalifa rests on piles and a thick raft, with a concrete spine, three buttressing wings and a shape built to confuse the wind.
