===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Dinosaurs were not always the rulers. For about 20 million years after they appeared, they stayed small and rare.

The land belonged to beaked plant-eaters called rhynchosaurs, to distant relatives of mammals, and to ancient cousins of crocodiles. Dinosaurs lived in their shadow.

Then, around 234 million years ago, volcanoes warmed the planet, and a long wet episode began. Plants changed, and most rhynchosaur lineages died out.

In the Dolomites of Italy, scientists studied dinosaur footprints. First there were no dinosaur tracks, and then there were many.

Some scientists think the dry conditions after the rain gave dinosaurs their chance. Others think their rise took many millions of years. No one knows the whole answer yet.

Either way, they went on to rule the land for more than 150 million years. The full story is on Miya's Lab.
