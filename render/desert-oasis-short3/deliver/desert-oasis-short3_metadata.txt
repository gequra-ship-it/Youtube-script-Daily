=== TITLE ===
The Underground Tunnels That Built Desert Civilizations

=== DESCRIPTION ===
How do you move water across a desert without losing it to the sun? About three thousand years ago, engineers in ancient Persia dug qanats, gently sloping tunnels that let gravity carry groundwater to the fields. The same idea appears as foggaras, falaj, karez and puquios, and oasis towns linked the Silk Road.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, which covers how oases get their water.
