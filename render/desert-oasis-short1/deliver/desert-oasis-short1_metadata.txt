=== TITLE ===
The Ancient Water Under the Sahara Is Running Out

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Beneath the Sahara lies water that scientists dated to between two hundred thousand and one million years old. It soaked into sandstone when the climate was very different, and today it is hardly being refilled. This Short follows fossil water from the green Sahara to the Nubian aquifer, krypton-81 dating, and Libya's Great Man-Made River.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, which covers how oases get their water.
