===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Some of the water under the Sahara has been underground for up to a million years. How did it get there?

Roughly eleven thousand to six thousand years ago, the Sahara was a green savanna, with lakes, rivers, elephants and hippos.

Rain from wet periods like this soaked deep into sandstone, rock full of tiny spaces that can hold water like a sponge.

Then the monsoon retreated, and the Sahara dried out. But the water stayed underground. Scientists call it fossil water.

In 2004, scientists dated water from the Nubian aquifer in Egypt using rare krypton atoms. Some was two hundred thousand to a million years old.

Where it reaches the surface, it can feed an oasis. But once it is pumped out, it will not be refilled in our lifetimes. The full story of how oases are made is on Miya's Lab.
