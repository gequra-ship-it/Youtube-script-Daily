supply chains to their knees.

not there yet.

the quiet darkness is
not empty space.

uses the energy of a world.
A Type Two uses
the energy of a star.
A Type Three uses
the energy of a galaxy.
We are still on
the first rung,
and the view from
here is humbling.
A single bad turn
of events could break
our networks, and yet
we keep climbing.

courage of the first step.
Every long journey through
the stars begins with
a single curious mind.
Our civilization is young,
and the scale is
a way to see
how far a young
civilization might grow
if it handles its
power with care.

is quiet, but it
is not closed.
It is simply waiting
for civilizations that
endure, and the first
