=== TITLE ===
Deepest Artificial Hole: Secrets Found 12km Below

=== DESCRIPTION ===
In 1970 Soviet scientists began drilling into the Arctic crust. They reached 12,262 meters and found plankton fossils, water in dry rock, hydrogen-boiling mud, and 180 degree heat that made solid granite creep like clay.
