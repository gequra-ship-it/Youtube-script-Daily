===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Most of the galaxies you can see through a telescope are already out of reach. Not for a while. Forever. Even a ship moving at the speed of light could never get there.

The universe is expanding. Space itself stretches, and the farther away a galaxy is, the faster it is carried away from us.

About 14 billion light-years out, that speed passes the speed of light. Einstein's limit is not broken, because nothing moves through space that fast. Space itself is growing.

At roughly 16 billion light-years lies the cosmic event horizon. A beam of light sent past it today would never arrive.

By one estimate, about 94 percent of the galaxies in the observable universe are already beyond it, and about 20,000 stars slip across every second.

So why can we never reach the edge of the universe? The full story is on Miya's Lab.
