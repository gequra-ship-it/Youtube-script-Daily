=== TITLE ===
How Wind, Faults and Floods Make a Desert Oasis

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Can wind dig a hole deep enough to reach water? In places like the Qattara Depression in Egypt, scientists think it did. This Short shows three ways water comes up in the desert: wind-dug hollows that reach the water table, faults that block groundwater like dams (the fan palms on the San Andreas), and flash floods that leave water under the gravel of a wadi.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, which covers how oases are made.
