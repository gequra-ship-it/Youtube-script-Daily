=== TITLE ===
The Search for Earth Two and the Secret of Alien Life

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Is there another Earth out there, and could it hold life? Astronomers have found thousands of planets, a few of them in the habitable zone of their stars, and the James Webb Space Telescope is now reading starlight that has passed through their atmospheres.

In this video, we follow the hunt from the first planet around a Sun-like star to Kepler-186f, Kepler-452b, Proxima Centauri b and the seven worlds of TRAPPIST-1, see how oxygen and methane could act as signs of life, and ask what the Fermi Paradox and the Great Filter might mean for us.
