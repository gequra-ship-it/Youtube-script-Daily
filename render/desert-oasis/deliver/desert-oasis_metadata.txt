=== TITLE ===
How Desert Oases Are Really Made

=== DESCRIPTION ===
In the driest deserts on Earth, clear springs and palm groves appear out of the sand. Where does that water come from? Some of it fell as rain in a green Sahara thousands of years ago, and some has been underground for hundreds of thousands of years.

In this video, we follow oasis water from distant mountains into stone aquifers, through artesian pressure, faults, wind-dug depressions and wadis, and look at the date palm, the qanat, the Silk Road, Libya's Great Man-Made River, fog nets and the risks of pumping fossil water.
