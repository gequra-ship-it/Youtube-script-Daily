=== TITLE ===
The Rain That Lasted a Million Years and Made the Dinosaurs

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Around 234 million years ago, volcanic eruptions warmed the planet and the supercontinent Pangaea entered a long wet age called the Carnian Pluvial Episode. When it ended, dinosaurs were on the rise.

In this video, we read the clues in red rocks, river sands and the oldest amber with trapped arthropods, follow the losses in the oceans and the birth of modern coral reefs, and look at why dinosaurs went from rare to everywhere, and why scientists still debate it.
