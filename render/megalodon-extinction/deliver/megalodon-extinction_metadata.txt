=== TITLE ===
Why the Greatest Ocean Giant Disappeared Forever

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Megalodon ruled the seas for millions of years, then vanished. Cooling seas, lost nurseries, migrating whales and smaller rivals are the leading ideas, and we weigh each as a hypothesis.
