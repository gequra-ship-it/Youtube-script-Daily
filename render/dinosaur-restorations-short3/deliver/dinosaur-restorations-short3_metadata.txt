How Scientists Found Dinosaur Colors in Fossils

=== DESCRIPTION ===
For a long time, the colors in paleoart were pure guesswork. Since 2010, tiny pigment structures called melanosomes have given scientists evidence for the colors of Sinosauropteryx, Anchiornis and Microraptor.
