=== TITLE ===
The Cosmic Prison: Why We Can Never Reach the Edge of the Universe

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Why can't we ever reach the edge of the universe? Space itself is expanding, and beyond about 16 billion light-years lies the cosmic event horizon. By one estimate, about 94 percent of the galaxies in the observable universe are already out of reach.

In this video, we go from Voyager 1 and the speed of light to Hubble's discovery, Hubble's law, the cosmic event horizon, dark energy, the observable universe, the Local Group and Andromeda, wormholes and warp drives, the shape of space, the Boötes Void, and the far future of the cosmos.
