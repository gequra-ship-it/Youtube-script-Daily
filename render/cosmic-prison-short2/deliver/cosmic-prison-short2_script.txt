===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Imagine being an astronomer who can never discover the Big Bang, no matter how hard you look.

Dark energy is speeding up the expansion of the universe. Over time, distant galaxies are carried beyond our cosmic horizon.

More than a trillion years from now, the sky above our merged home galaxy, nicknamed Milkdromeda, will show no other galaxies at all. Just one island of stars, floating in an empty black ocean.

Even the afterglow of the Big Bang, the cosmic microwave background, will be stretched beyond detection.

Physicists Lawrence Krauss and Robert Scherrer argued that astronomers in that era might conclude the universe is static and eternal. They would have no reason to suspect that the universe was ever expanding. The real history would be erased.

We live in the brief window when the evidence is still visible. The full story is on Miya's Lab.
