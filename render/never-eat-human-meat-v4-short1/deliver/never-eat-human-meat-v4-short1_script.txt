===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Can you cook away every danger in meat? For one strange particle, the answer is no. Even 600 degrees Celsius was not always enough.

It is called a prion. It is not a virus, and it is not a bacterium. It is a protein folded the wrong way, and it can force normal proteins in your brain to fold the same wrong way.

Normal cooking kills germs like Salmonella. Prions can survive standard hospital sterilization, and formalin does not reliably destroy them.

Your immune system barely notices. A prion is made from a protein your own body makes, so there is no fever and no antibody response.

Then it waits. In the kuru epidemic of Papua New Guinea, symptoms sometimes appeared more than fifty years after exposure.

How a funeral ritual spread this disease, and how the survivors' genes fought back, is the full story on Miya's Lab.
