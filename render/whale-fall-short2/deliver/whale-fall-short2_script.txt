===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

When a whale dies in the deep sea, its body can feed life in the dark for decades. Scientists call it a whale fall.

First come the scavengers: sleeper sharks, hagfish, crabs, and giant isopods. Together they can strip away 40 to 60 kilograms of flesh every single day.

Within about a year and a half, only the skeleton is left. Then bristle worms, snails, and tiny crustaceans swarm the bones and the mud around them, far more than on the ordinary seafloor.

Next, bacteria break down the fat inside the bones and release hydrogen sulfide. Other bacteria use that gas as energy, instead of sunlight. The skeleton becomes a kind of hot spring, fueled by bone.

One study found about 185 species on a single skeleton, more than the richest hot spring vent field known at the time.

This stage can last 50 years or more. The full story is on Miya's Lab.
