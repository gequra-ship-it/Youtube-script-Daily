===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Can you cook away every danger in meat? For one strange particle, the answer is no.

It is called a prion. It is not a virus, and it is not a bacterium. It is a protein that folds the wrong way.

Ordinary germs like Salmonella are killed by normal cooking heat. Prions are far tougher. In lab studies, some infectivity remained even after heating to 600 degrees Celsius.

Your immune system does not help either. A prion is made from a protein your own body makes, so it does not look foreign. Researchers see no antibody response.

And then there is the waiting. Kuru could stay silent for more than fifty years. Someone could feel healthy for decades, and still fall ill much later.

Once symptoms begin, kuru has been fatal. The full story of how it was discovered is on Miya's Lab.
