=== TITLE ===
Why Did the Rulers of Ancient Seas Vanish?

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Trilobites ruled the seas for about 270 million years, with eyes made of crystal and armor that rolled into a ball. How did they survive three mass extinctions, and what finally ended them?
