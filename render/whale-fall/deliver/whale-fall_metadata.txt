=== TITLE ===
What Happens When a Whale Dies in the Deep Sea

=== DESCRIPTION ===
When a whale dies and sinks, its body can feed life in the dark deep sea for decades. Follow a whale fall from the surface to the abyss and through every stage of an ecosystem built on one carcass.

In this video, we meet sleeper sharks, hagfish and giant isopods, the bone-eating Osedax worms with no mouth and no gut, bacteria that turn bone fat into chemical energy, fossil traces 30 to 100 million years old, and what whaling took from the deep ocean.
