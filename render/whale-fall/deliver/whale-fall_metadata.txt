=== TITLE ===
The Whale That Feeds an Ocean Floor

=== DESCRIPTION ===
When a whale dies and sinks to the deep seafloor, it feeds a whole ecosystem for decades. Follow a whale fall through its four stages, from sharks and hagfish to zombie worms and bone reefs.
