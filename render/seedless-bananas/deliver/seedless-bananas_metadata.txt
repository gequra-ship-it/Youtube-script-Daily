=== TITLE ===
The Secret Life of Bananas: How Do Seedless Bananas Even Exist?

=== DESCRIPTION ===
How can a fruit with no seeds spread across the whole world? Bananas are nearly sterile plants that people grow by cloning, and that one fact hides a surprising chain of stories: a famous banana that the world largely lost, a fungus that now threatens the one we eat, and a slightly radioactive snack.

In this video, we follow the banana from its wild, seed-filled ancestors to triploid chromosomes, parthenocarpy, suckers and tissue culture, then uncover three secrets: Big Mike and Panama disease, the spread of TR4, and the science of potassium-40, plus berries, banana republics, ripening rooms, and the frozen gene bank in Belgium.
