=== TITLE ===
Why Water Is Like Concrete at 200 km/h

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Would you aim for a lake or a forest if you fell from the sky? At about two hundred kilometers per hour, water can behave much like a hard surface, while trees and deep snow stretch the stop. This Short explains stopping distance with the cases of Vesna Vulovic and Juliane Koepcke.
