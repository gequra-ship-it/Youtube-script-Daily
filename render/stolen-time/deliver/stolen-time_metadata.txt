=== TITLE ===
Three Hours Equals Twenty-Three Years

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Could three hours on an ocean planet really equal twenty-three years on Earth? Gravity, spinning black holes and relativity say it is possible on paper, though no such planet has ever been found.
