=== TITLE ===
The Alien on Earth: The Bizarre Evolution and Genius of the Octopus

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Could the closest thing to an alien mind be living in our own oceans? The octopus has blue blood, three hearts, and most of its neurons spread through eight arms, and it edits the RNA messages of its own nerve cells.

In this video, we follow the octopus from a worm-like ancestor about six hundred million years ago to its shell-less, flexible body, then look at camouflage, tool use, the Inky escape, RNA editing, mimicry, and the short life that may explain why octopuses never built a culture.
