=== TITLE ===
How Our Pictures of Dinosaurs Kept Getting It Wrong

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Iguanodon once had a horn on its nose, Elasmosaurus had its head on its tail, and Hallucigenia was drawn upside down and backwards. For 170 years, scientists and artists have kept redrawing extinct animals as new fossils turned up.

In this video, we follow paleoart from the Crystal Palace sculptures and the Bone Wars to swamp-bound sauropods, tail-dragging T. rex, the Dinosaur Renaissance, the many lives of Spinosaurus, shrink-wrapping, lips, feathers and the first real dinosaur colors.
