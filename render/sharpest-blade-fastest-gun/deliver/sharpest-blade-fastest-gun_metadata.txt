Science's Sharpest Blade and Fastest Gun

=== DESCRIPTION ===
The sharpest blade ends in a single atom, and the fastest gun fires protons at nearly the speed of light. A tour of obsidian, diamond knives, light-gas guns and the Large Hadron Collider.
