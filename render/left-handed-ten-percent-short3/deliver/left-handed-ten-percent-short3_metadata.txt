=== TITLE ===
Hands on the Cave Wall: The Hidden Left-Handers

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ice Age hand stencils, scratches on 400,000-year-old teeth and forced hand-switching in schools: the story of how left-handers have been counted, and hidden, for tens of thousands of years. About three quarters of more than 500 cave stencils in France and Spain are left hands, which fits a right-handed painter, and roughly nine in ten Neanderthals seem to have been right-handed.

Surveys of Americans born around 1900 found only about 3 percent left-handed; for those born after 1950 it is roughly 11 to 12 percent, because children were no longer forced to hide it. The full video on Miya's Lab has the rest.
