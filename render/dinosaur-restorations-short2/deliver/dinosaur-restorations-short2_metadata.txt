Spinosaurus: The Dinosaur Redrawn Again and Again

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Spinosaurus fossils found in Egypt were described in 1915 and destroyed in a Munich bombing in 1944. New fossils in 2014 and a paddle-like tail in 2020 changed the picture, and scientists are still arguing about how it lived.
