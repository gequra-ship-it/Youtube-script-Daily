===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

This worm eats whale skeletons, and it has no mouth, no gut, and no anus. So how does it eat?

In 2002, researchers found it on a dead gray whale almost 2,900 meters deep. They named it Osedax, which means bone devourer.

Above the bone, it waves feathery red plumes that take oxygen from the water. Below, it pushes roots into the bone itself. It does not swallow its food. It grows into it.

The roots release acid that wears the bone away, and bacteria inside them break down collagen, the tough protein in bone.

And the worms you see are all females. Up to 100 microscopic males can live inside the tube of one female.

How one dead whale feeds the deep sea for decades is the full story on Miya's Lab. Wherever bone reaches the deep seafloor, these little red flowers seem to find it.
