=== TITLE ===
The Worm That Eats Whale Bones With No Mouth

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Osedax, the bone-eating worm, has no mouth and no gut. It grows roots into whale bones, dissolves them with acid and lets bacteria digest the collagen inside.
