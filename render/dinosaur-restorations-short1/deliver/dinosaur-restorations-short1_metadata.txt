=== TITLE ===
The Dinosaur With a Horn on Its Nose

=== DESCRIPTION ===
For decades, Iguanodon was pictured with a horn on its nose. Gideon Mantell named it in 1825 from a handful of teeth, the Crystal Palace sculptures showed a heavy four-legged beast in 1854, and the Bernissart skeletons in 1878 moved the horn to the thumb.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, which follows how pictures of extinct animals changed.
