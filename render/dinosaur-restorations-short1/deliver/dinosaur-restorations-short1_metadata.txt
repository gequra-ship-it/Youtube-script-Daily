=== TITLE ===
The Dinosaur Horn That Was Actually a Thumb

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Iguanodon was drawn with a horn on its nose for decades, including in London's 1854 Crystal Palace sculptures. Skeletons found in a Belgian coal mine in 1878 showed the spike was a thumb.
