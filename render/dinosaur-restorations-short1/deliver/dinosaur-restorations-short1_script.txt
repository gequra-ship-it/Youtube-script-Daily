===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Why did the best scientists once put a horn on a dinosaur's nose? In a park in south London stands a strange, bulky creature made of concrete, with a horn on its nose. More than a hundred and seventy years ago, this was what they thought a dinosaur looked like. Where did that horn come from?

The story begins in the 1820s in Sussex, England, with a doctor named Gideon Mantell. Mantell noticed that they looked like the teeth of a modern iguana, only much bigger. So he imagined the animal as a giant lizard, and in 1825 he named it Iguanodon, meaning iguana tooth.

Among the bones Mantell later studied was a cone-shaped spike. He guessed that it was a horn, and in his sketches it ended up on the tip of the animal's nose, like a rhinoceros. Early pictures showed Iguanodon as a huge four-legged lizard with a spike on its snout. It was also completely wrong.

In 1854, the artist Benjamin Waterhouse Hawkins unveiled the world's first life-sized dinosaur sculptures in the grounds of the Crystal Palace, working with the anatomist Richard Owen. Owen saw dinosaurs as advanced reptiles with sturdy, almost mammal-like legs, so the sculptures look like heavy, elephant-shaped lizards.

The horn was finally explained in 1878, deep in a coal mine at Bernissart in Belgium. Miners found dozens of Iguanodon skeletons, many of them nearly complete. When the scientist Louis Dollo studied them, the mystery spike turned up on the hand. It was a thumb spike, not a nose horn. And the skeletons suggested an animal that could rise up on its strong hind legs, not a lizard sprawled on four.

Put the three versions side by side. In 1825, Mantell named Iguanodon from a handful of teeth and imagined a giant iguana. In 1854, the Crystal Palace sculpture showed a heavy four-legged beast with a horn on its nose. And after the Bernissart skeletons were found in 1878, the horn moved to the thumb, and the animal stood up on its hind legs.

Mantell's horn was not a failure, but a first attempt. The full story of how pictures of extinct animals changed is on Miya's Lab.
