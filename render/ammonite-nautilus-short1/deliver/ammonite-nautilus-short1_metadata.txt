=== TITLE ===
Ammonites Were Not Nautilus Relatives

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Ammonites and the nautilus have almost identical spiral shells, but they were not close relatives. Their shells hide different designs, from the siphuncle to the folded chamber walls called sutures.
