=== TITLE ===
Paleozoic Giants Were Smaller Than the Legends

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Books once said Dunkleosteus reached nine meters, Cameroceras nine meters and Anomalocaris a meter. Newer estimates put them at about three and a half meters, about two meters and under forty centimeters. This Short looks at how the numbers changed and what Anomalocaris could really eat.

Based on the fact-checked script of the full video on Miya's Lab, a journey through the Paleozoic Era.
