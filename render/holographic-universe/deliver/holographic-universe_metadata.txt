=== TITLE ===
Is Our Entire Universe Just a Hologram?

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Could our three-dimensional world be described by information on a distant two-dimensional surface? This is the holographic principle, an idea born from black holes. No one has proven that our universe works this way, and this video separates what is established from what is still a hypothesis.

In this video, we follow the story from the hologram on a credit card to black hole entropy, Hawking radiation, the information paradox, 't Hooft and Susskind, Maldacena's holographic duality, entanglement and space, the Page curve, and the experiments that tried to test it.
