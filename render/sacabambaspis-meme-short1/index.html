Two wide, unblinking eyes
stare out of a
shocked fish face in
a Helsinki museum.
The real story behind
it is stranger than the joke.
That fish is Sacabambaspis,
and the face is
a museum model in
the Finnish Museum of
Natural History.
In August 2022,
a photo of it
spread on Twitter,
mostly as a joke
about a badly made
reconstruction.
About a year later,
Japanese users found that
post, and the fish took off.
They made fan art,
stickers, and plush toys.
Many people in Japan
simply fell in love
with its bewildered look.
But the animal was real.
Sacabambaspis was a jawless
fish that lived in
Ordovician seas, roughly
450 million years ago,
long before the first
dinosaur.
Its fossils come from
the Anzaldo Formation near
Cochabamba in Bolivia.
In 1986, the paleontologists
Pierre-Yves Gagnier, Alain
Blieck, and Gonzalo Rodrigo
named it Sacabambaspis
janvieri.
The name combines the
nearby village of Sacabamba
with the Greek word
aspis, meaning shield.
The species name honors
the French paleontologist
Philippe Janvier.
It was small.
An adult was about
twenty-five centimeters
long, roughly the length
of a shoe.
Its front half was
covered by bony armor.
One large shield covered
the back of the
head and body,
and another covered the belly.
Behind that armor trailed
a flexible tail covered
in small scales.
Now the eyes.
In most modern fish,
the eyes sit on
the sides of the
head, which gives a
wide view for spotting
predators.
Sacabambaspis had its eyes
set toward the front
of the head.
So the meme got
that part at least
partly right.
Scientists do not agree
on exactly what this
layout was for.
What about the shocked mouth?
That is the part
to be careful with.
Reconstructing a fish from
flattened, broken fossils
is hard, and a
sculptor has to guess
what the soft parts
looked like.
Scientists today usually
draw Sacabambaspis with
a more streamlined head.
The meme face is
one artist's guess,
not a photograph of the past.
So when you see
that famous face,
remember two things.
The fish was real,
and the look on
its face was partly a guess.
The goofy face was
never the whole story.
