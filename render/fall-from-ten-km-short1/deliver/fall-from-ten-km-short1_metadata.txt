What Happens When You Fall from Ten Kilometers?

=== DESCRIPTION ===
What happens to a body that falls from ten thousand meters? Thin air, freezing cold, and a fall of about two hundred kilometers per hour are only the beginning. This Short follows the first minutes of the fall and the surfaces that decide who walks away.
