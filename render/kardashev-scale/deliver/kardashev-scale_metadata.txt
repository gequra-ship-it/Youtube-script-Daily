=== TITLE ===
How Powerful Is Humanity in the Cosmos? The Kardashev Scale Explained

=== DESCRIPTION ===
How powerful is humanity when measured against the cosmos? In the 1960s, Soviet astrophysicist Nikolai Kardashev proposed ranking civilizations by the energy they can command, and Carl Sagan's estimate puts humanity at roughly Type zero point seven.

In this video, we walk the Kardashev ladder from a Type One planet to a Type Two star and a Type Three galaxy, look at Freeman Dyson's solar-collector swarm, and ask whether the silence of the cosmos could point to a Great Filter that civilizations must survive.
