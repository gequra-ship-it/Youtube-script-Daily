=== TITLE ===
How Powerful Is Humanity? The Kardashev Scale

=== DESCRIPTION ===
How strong is humanity compared with the universe? Soviet astronomer Nikolai Kardashev ranked civilizations by the energy they use, from a planet to a star to a galaxy, and we sit around Type 0.7.
