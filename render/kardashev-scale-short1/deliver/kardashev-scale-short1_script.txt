===== 1부: TTS 입력용 전문 (장면당 한 문단) =====

Look at your phone screen right now. It talks to satellites, moves information across the planet, and lights up with a single tap. Because of our modern cities and rockets, most people assume humanity is an advanced civilization. But on the cosmic scale, how powerful are we really?

In nineteen sixty-four, Soviet astrophysicist Nikolai Kardashev proposed a way to rank civilizations by the energy they can use. In the popular version of his scale, a Type One civilization commands the entire energy of its home planet, every gust of wind, every ocean tide, every beam of sunlight that reaches the ground. A Type Two commands the entire output of its parent star. A Type Three commands the power of a whole galaxy.

So where does Earth stand? Astronomer Carl Sagan estimated that humanity sits around Type zero point seven. That is a score below one. We are still climbing toward the first step of the ladder, and we have not reached it yet.

Think about what that means for your daily life. Most of our energy still comes from digging up the remains of ancient life and burning it. We cannot stop a category five hurricane from battering our coastlines. In eighteen fifteen, the eruption of Mount Tambora cooled the planet so much that the following year became known as the year without a summer.

Space can reach us too. In March of nineteen eighty-nine, a solar storm blacked out the power grid across Quebec. We live on a planet we only partly understand, and we cannot control it.

A true Type One civilization would be different. In theory, with a whole planet's worth of power, it might ease earthquakes, steer the weather, and keep droughts from taking hold. It would not be a helpless victim of planetary weather. It would treat its world like a carefully tuned home.

We have built microchips, walked on the Moon, and created artificial intelligence. Yet one bad day of space weather or one volcanic eruption can still set us back. We are crossing one of the most fragile stretches of the climb, the leap from Type Zero to Type One. How long will it take us to master our own world without burning it down?
