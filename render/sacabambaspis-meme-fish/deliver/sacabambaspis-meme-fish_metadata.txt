=== TITLE ===
The Bizarre Biology of Sacabambaspis, the Meme Fish

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Why does a 460-million-year-old fish look so surprised? Sacabambaspis was a small armored fish with no jaws, no side fins, and eyes at the very front of its head, and a hand-made museum model turned it into an internet star.

In this video, we separate the meme from the fossils: where the fish lived, how its armor and mouth worked, what a mass-death site in Bolivia tells us, how jaws later changed the sea, and why this cousin of ours still matters.
