=== TITLE ===
How a Tiny Beetle Larva Turns Frogs Into Its Meal

=== DESCRIPTION ===
How can a larva about two centimeters long beat a frog? Follow the Epomis ground beetle, whose young lure amphibians, dodge the tongue, and feed on the animal that came to eat them.

In this video, we look at what two Israeli researchers filmed in the lab, how the larva's lure and hooked jaws work, what the adult beetle does at night, and which questions scientists have not yet answered. We also separate what is confirmed from what is still a hypothesis.
